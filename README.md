# news-image-20261126 - one image

An entry for make.fun's [Sum up 26 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126) bounty.

The image: a Long March rocket lifts off at dawn carrying Shenzhou-24 toward the Tiangong space station, with the flags of China and Pakistan flying side by side at the launch site and the date captioned at the bottom. On 26 November 2026 China launched Shenzhou-24 with Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali aboard, the first foreign astronaut to fly to China's space station.

`main.py` validates the bundled `entry.png` (a real PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MiB, at most 1024 by 1024 pixels) and copies it to `/output/entry.png`, checking the copy matches byte for byte.
